# Voltaic Bros LLC — Internal Pricing Memo

Prepared for internal use by the estimating team. Distribution outside the company is not authorized.

## Service Tiers

**Basic — $450 flat rate.** Covers single-circuit diagnostics, one outlet or switch replacement, and a standard safety inspection. No panel work included.

**Standard — $950 flat rate.** Includes up to 4 circuits and panel labeling or surge protection, plus a full inspection report and a 90-day workmanship warranty.

**Premium — $1,800 flat rate.** Full panel upgrade evaluation, up to 8 circuits, dedicated circuit installation, and a 1-year workmanship warranty.

A 10% discount applies automatically to any invoice at $5,000 and above in total contracted work, per our standing coupon policy. Coupons do not stack with seasonal promotions.

## Worked Example

A recent job combined one Standard-tier service call with additional labor and materials:

| Item | Amount |
|---|---|
| Standard tier service | $950 |
| Additional labor (2 hrs @ $110/hr) | $220 |
| Materials (breaker, wire, labels) | $180 |
| **Total Due** | **$1,500** |

The customer paid the deposit at signing and the balance on completion, per standard Terms below.

## Terms

Deposit is 30% due at signing, with the remaining balance due on completion of work. Invoices unpaid after 15 days accrue a 1.5% monthly late fee. Warranty claims must be filed in writing within the window stated per tier above, and are void if a non-Voltaic electrician has touched the panel in the interim.

## Cash Flow

For scheduling and crew allocation, we plan cash flow assuming a 50% deposit collected up front on each job — for example, a $2,000 contracted job nets a $1,000 deposit at signing — with the remaining balance collected at completion. This lets us cover materials cost on larger jobs without carrying float past the first week.

## Notes

Pricing is reviewed quarterly against material cost trends. Any tier price change requires sign-off from both partners before it takes effect on new quotes.
